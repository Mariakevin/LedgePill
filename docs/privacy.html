Privacy policy

Privacy policy.

LedgePill is a native Dynamic Island app for Linux, Windows and Android. This policy covers all platforms; the Android app is the one submitted to Google Play.

What we collect

Nothing. No personal data is collected, transmitted, or stored on any server. Everything stays on your device.

What the app reads and why

  • Notifications are mirrored passively: titles are read only to display in the island, and are never sent anywhere.
  • Media track title, artist, album and artwork via MPRIS, the system media session, or media sessions — shown on an on-screen card only.
  • System state (volume, battery, network, Bluetooth, weather) is read locally and displayed locally.
  • Webhooks: loopback-only HTTP on 127.0.0.1, with a bearer token stored locally with owner-only permissions. Only local scripts you authorize can reach it.

Network requests the app makes

  • Weather: your place name to wttr.in (no personal data).
  • Lyrics: song title and artist to lrclib.net (nothing personal).
  • Information feeds (RSS/Atom/JSON) from URLs you configure.
  • AI assistant (opt-in, disabled by default): when enabled with a remote provider (Claude, Gemini, OpenRouter), live device context can be shared with that provider; local providers (Ollama, LM Studio) stay on device. Destructive actions always require confirmation.
  • Developer activity: process names only (Claude Code, Codex, Aider, OpenCode, Goose). Prompts and command text are never retained or displayed.

What we don’t do

No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third-party SDKs, no account creation, and no data sold or shared with third parties.

Storage

Config, notification history (up to 50 entries, owner-only permissions), the webhook token, and the task list are all local files. “Clear” removes notification history.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and no data is collected from anyone.

Changes

This policy may update; the “Last updated” date below reflects the latest revision.
