A small island with a lot on its mind.

Everything is one gesture away — no windows to hunt for, no tray menu to dig through.

🎵 Media, up close

Album art, transport controls, a seekable progress bar and a deck of pill faces: now playing, clock and battery, the current synced lyric, or just the title.

🔋 System awareness

Volume and brightness OSD, a battery ring, network and Bluetooth state, weather, notifications, information feeds and webhook messages.

🍅 Quietly useful

A pomodoro timer with focus tasks, mute indicators, quick toggles, live system stats, developer activity and a local AI assistant.

Take it off the browser.

The real thing sits on your desktop, follows your media player and keeps quiet until something happens.
